===== WELCOME =====

Hello. My name is Austin C Kampen. I am currently writing this in the fall of 2026 in St. Louis, Missouri (where I was born, raised, educated, and currently work) in the United States of America (although, I struggle with calling it "United" at this point in time). It feels as we are in a very divisive time in this country. But I was also born in the 1990's so I don't remember the civil war or any other points in America's history where we were fighting with our neighbors. Or maybe it never went away and was just dormant or was ignored while the overall quality of life improved for most Americans. But I am not a historian so I will stop guessing. So why am I typing this right now? Honestly I am not 100% sure. I feel a drive within me to complete this project. The real story starts 2 years ago in the fall of 2024 when I was meditating in a chair and looking at the trees outside my apartment. I said to the universe, or any god/entity that would listen, "if you have anything to tell me, I am listening". I proceeded to write some things down in a small notebook. I had 2 more experiences after that, each one stronger than the previous. After these experiences, I felt a compulsion to share the words I wrote with those I trusted. So over the course of a few weeks I took pictures of the pages, typed them up, put in some extra commentary, an intro, an outro, and created something I called "Make It Happen" based on the cover of the journal I wrote in (I scratched out the "make" during my first experience). That was the 1.0 version. I made some small changes, and paid to have it printed, 3-hole punched, and put in binders (which turns out is actually pretty pricy but this felt more important than money at the time). That was the 1.1 version. I then had meals with those I trusted most, and gave them the binders so I could sort of explain it in person. That was the 1.1 version. Then I edited it down a little more, keeping the core message of it, and put that on some flash drives which I gave to those that I had fair amounts of trust in. That was the 1.2 version. Instead of version 1.3 being that same document but for the public (my original plan), I wanted to create something new. So I created this instead.

At this point I want to make some things clear about what I think happened and what I think about myself.
First, I want to address the skeptics. The scientists, analysts, and those who always follow logic and reasoning. I will admit, I do not know what happened. I know it felt like something or someone talking to me, and later 2 of them writing through my hand. We even wrote some things back and forth to each other. That is the best way I can describe it. But as I type that out, I think about how I would view a friend who told me this story. I would think they had some underlying mental health issues and maybe took some illicit substances or something. In this age right now we are learning more about psychology and how our brains work. We are also learning more about physics and how reality might not be what we think it is. Is there a possibility there really is something beyond our physical world? Other consciousnesses? And is that something we could ever scientifically prove? I think we still have so much more to learn about the human mind, consciousness, reality, and more. The more we learn, the more we realize we really don't know anything (at least not to a significant degree). So I ask you not toss this aside as the ramblings of a mental patient, and see if anything resonates with you. Maybe nothing will. And that's ok too. Try to think less and feel more when (or if) you read this.

Next I want to speak to those who feel spiritually open to the secrets of the universe. Even divine truths told to us by entities more wise than we could ever hope to understand, go through the filter of our own consciousness and experience. So nothing written or said by any one is perfect and infallible. I am just a human. There is nothing special about me other than I listened. And anyone can. I asked the being(s) during my experiences how I could tell the Truth from untruth. They told me the Truth will be something that is good for not only you, but for everyone around you. The Truth is healing. The Truth is loving. The Truth is forgiving. If you feel you are being told, or someone preaches they have been told, truths that are full of anger, jealousy, or are about looking unfavorably upon a certain group of people, I would caution you to not listen to those voices. I think those voices are our human brain filter and humanity projecting onto the Truth what we desire for it to be. So with that said, do not take the words here as infallible. My experience is my own and all I can speak to. All of the major faiths of the world today have scriptures that were not written by the prophets/sages/gods/etc. themselves, but rather by their disciples at later points in time. I am not saying I am a prophet, but I am also not denying it either. I simply want this to just be what it is. I have been told something and given a strong desire to share it with those I love and the world as a whole. It may feel tempting to go all in with this and believe every word, but also remember this is them through my filter and I am flawed and have biases and lack experiences other have had. So the viewpoint is limited. So find the core meanings, and feel how they resonate through your own filter. Try to rely less on what you feel (if I am speaking to you in this section, I think you are very in touch with spiritual things already) and more on your own worldly mind and life experiences and how they are reflected in these words. The only hidden messages in this text are the ones you create for yourself. Those messages are by you and for you. Then process what you've found then share it with others that are close to you and see if they relate to it. Someone will, and they might really need to hear it.

Back to what this is. Less than a year after my first experience, on June 18th, 2025, I started writing in an old journal my parents had gifted me. Inside the cover my mom wrote:

For our Beloved Son, Austin, as you begin a new chapter in life.
January 1, 2022.
Love*, Mom & Dad
(*forever and always)

I had not used this journal since they gave it to me, but I found it recently and decided to use it. So I named it "The Book of Kampen" and wrote in it. I was not being channeled for these writings as I felt I was with the previous ones. This is just what I have written based on my thoughts meditating on my experiences in an attempt to condense the Truth, filtered through me, down to something that is more easily understood. My attempt is to connect people with this rather than divide. I believe if you read this with an open mind and an open heart, you will receive the message you need to from it. I do not know what that message will be. Only you will.


Note: I have a lot of quotes in this. I tried to pull from various sources (academic, religious, cultural, etc) in an attempt to show that others have seen parts of these truths as well across time. And I apologize if I quoted any person or group that has substantial moral failings. I am going by the words and the thoughts behind them rather than the person (or persons) themselves. Please try to look past it if you can.

Castlewood State Park
